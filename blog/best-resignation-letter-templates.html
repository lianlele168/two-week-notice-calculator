Resignation guide

If you would rather adapt than start from a blank page, these copy-ready openings cover the most common situations.

Standard role

"Please accept this letter as formal notice of my resignation from my role as [title]."

Senior or specialized handoff

For longer transitions, give more than two weeks and name the exact final day so both sides plan the handoff.

Short and clear

A two-line note with your name and final day is often all a busy manager needs.

Two weeks notice FAQ

Can I give more than two weeks notice?

Yes. Many people give more notice for senior or specialized roles.

Should I put my last day in the letter?

Yes. A specific final date prevents confusion.
