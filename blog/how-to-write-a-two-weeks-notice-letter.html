How to Write a Two Weeks Notice Letter

Resignation guide

A two weeks notice letter does not need to be long. Keep it brief, professional, and specific about your final day so there is no confusion on either side.

Start with the basics

State who you are, your manager's name, and your final working day. A simple opening such as "Please accept this letter as formal notice of my resignation" is enough for most workplaces.

Name the final day clearly

Instead of only writing "two weeks notice," put the exact date. This prevents mix-ups and gives your employer a clean transition timeline.

Keep it short

One or two sentences of thanks is plenty. You do not need to explain why you are leaving unless you want to.

Two weeks notice FAQ

Do I have to give two weeks notice?

No. It is a professional courtesy, not a universal legal rule. Check your contract if the stakes are high.

What if my last day falls on a weekend?

Pick the closest regular workday and confirm it with your manager.
